# AI tool: 5 questions before you pick one

From @techlordsbywlm ("AI tool chunne se pehle 5 sawaal"). Price is the smallest question. Before your team puts client files, code or customer data into a new AI tool, get a written answer to these five, from the tool's own docs, terms or admin settings. "Paid" does not automatically mean "private".

Copy the table at the bottom and fill it in for each tool you compare.

## 1. Is my data used for training? Where is the setting?

- [ ] Find the setting that controls whether your chats and files are used to train or improve models, and what it is set to by default **on the plan you are buying**.
- [ ] Check personal, team and business plans separately. They often have different terms.
- [ ] Example (Anthropic, 28 Aug 2025): on Claude Free, Pro and Max, the user chooses whether chats are used for model training and can change it in Privacy Settings; choosing yes extends retention to five years, otherwise retention stays at 30 days. Claude for Work (Team, Enterprise) and the API are under different, commercial terms.
- [ ] How long is data kept after you delete a chat or a file?

## 2. Can I export everything?

- [ ] Is there an export for chats, files, projects and settings? In what format?
- [ ] On team plans, who can run it: every member, or only an admin or owner?
- [ ] Try one export **now**, before you need it. Example: Claude exports from Settings → Privacy → Export data on web or desktop (not the mobile apps); the download link arrives by email and expires after 24 hours.

## 3. How does the bill grow?

- [ ] Per seat, per usage (tokens, credits, requests), or both?
- [ ] What happens at the limit: blocked, slowed, or charged extra automatically?
- [ ] Can you set a spending limit and an alert? Who gets the alert?
- [ ] Price for your team in six months, not for one person today.

## 4. Lock-in: if the tool shuts down, where is my work?

- [ ] Your code lives in **your** git repository, not only in the tool's cloud workspace.
- [ ] Important decisions, prompts and specs are in files you own, not only in chat history.
- [ ] Is there a public shutdown or data-deletion policy? How much notice do customers get?
- [ ] Could you switch to another tool next month without losing anything?

## 5. Who can see the chats?

- [ ] Can team admins or owners read members' chats and files?
- [ ] What does a shared link expose, and to whom? Can shared links be turned off?
- [ ] Do integrations (drive, email, code host) give the tool access to more than you meant?
- [ ] Is single sign-on available, and can you remove a member's access the day they leave?

## Before the client files go in

- [ ] All five have written answers from the tool's own docs, terms or settings.
- [ ] The settings are checked in the actual account, not only in the docs.
- [ ] The team knows what never goes in (passwords, keys, IDs, salaries, client files without permission).
